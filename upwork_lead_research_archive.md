Status: KILL AS PRIMARY SKU — REFRAME AS INPUT LAYER

Task family:

Lead Research / Qualified Lead List

## Final Interpretation

Lead Research is not dead.

But as a standalone primary SKU, it is weak.

The evidence from Batch 1 and Batch 2 shows a stable structure:

- L1/L2 lead list work is dense, but low-priced and commoditized.
- L3 value exists, but once pricing improves, the buyer usually purchases downstream execution: outreach, SDR work, campaign operations, automation, meetings, recruiting outcomes, or closing.

## Evidence Pattern

Included standalone research/list examples:

- 100 ICP leads for $50 fixed.
- Strategic B2B lead research at $5-$12/hr.
- Storeleads ecommerce filtering with unknown price and paid-database dependency.
- Real estate agency decision-maker contact list at $8-$10/hr.

Excluded higher-value adjacent examples:

- Outbound Sales Leader at $25-$50/hr: value comes from outreach sequences and discovery calls.
- Clay/Apollo/Instantly VA: value comes from sales ops and campaign execution.
- Beta Rep recruiting: value comes from outreach and securing install/case-study outcomes.
- SDR roles: value comes from meetings and qualification calls.
- Closer roles: value comes from conversion/closing.

## Current Decision

Lead Research / Qualified Lead List:

- KILL as first primary Upwork SKU.
- Keep as an input layer for higher-value offers.

## Reframe

Lead data may still be valuable inside:

- Account Intelligence
- Sales Opportunity Research
- Market Entry Research with potential customer list
- Buyer signal research
- Target account prioritization

The valuable layer is not:

Name + Email + LinkedIn

The valuable layer may be:

Which accounts are worth contacting, why now, who matters, what changed, and what angle to use.

## Strategic Learning

Public-Source Comparative Research:

- Value per task can be decent.
- Market density appears weak.

Lead Research:

- Market density is high.
- Standalone research value is weak.

The next target should sit between these:

- close enough to revenue to matter
- judgment-heavy enough to avoid commodity pricing
- not so far downstream that the work becomes SDR/campaign/closing execution
