Status: NEXT SCAN — REMOTE SUPPLIER DISCOVERY + VERIFICATION + COMPARISON

Objective:

Test whether buyers purchase a remote, low-asset supplier research deliverable with enough density and pricing to become a productization candidate.

## Strict Target

Buyer wants:

- specific product/category
- supplier/vendor discovery
- qualification against specs
- verification of supplier fit
- MOQ / quote / lead time / certification details where possible
- comparison table
- recommendation or ranked shortlist

Buyer does not primarily want:

- local agent
- factory visit
- sample inspection / QC
- negotiation as main outcome
- procurement execution
- long-term supplier management
- import/export logistics ownership
- regulated compliance consulting as the core value

## Strict-Fit Rule

Include only if:

- Execution Dependency is `None` or `Light outreach`.
- Research Share of Value is `High`.
- Final deliverable is a structured artifact: supplier shortlist, RFQ/quote matrix, MOQ/lead-time/certification comparison, ranked recommendation.

- AI Completion Notes
- Entry Barrier Notes
- Include / Exclude Decision
- Exclusion Reason

## Decision Target

Look for 8-12 strict-fit records with recurring structure:

- 5-10 suppliers
- verified contacts
- MOQ
- quote / indicative price
- lead time
- certification / capability proof
- comparison table
- recommendation

If this repeatedly appears at $100-$300+ or equivalent hourly, mark as close to:

PASS — Productization Candidate

If strict-fit records are sparse and most value comes from execution, mark:

WATCH or KILL / REFRAME
