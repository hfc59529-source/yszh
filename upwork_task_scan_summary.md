## Simple Priority

Formula used: Demand x Price x AI Leverage / Entry Barrier, scored qualitatively from this 50-job sample.

| Rank | Task | Why |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Competitor / funnel / market teardown for SMBs | Repeated across competitor analysis, mystery shopping, SEO/social audits, benchmark testing, and one high-ticket intelligence job. Deliverables are report-shaped and AI-assisted. Does not always require 10-year industry credentials if framed as structured research. |
| 2 | B2B lead list + qualification / enrichment | Highest repeat demand and very AI-assisted. Buyers often have spend/hiring history. Weakness: many low-budget jobs and tool access requirements. Best only if packaged around a narrow niche and quality/verification. |
| 3 | Market entry / feasibility brief | Clear recurring need with $200-$500 center, but some buyers expect expert business modeling. Good SKU if narrowed to "decision brief" rather than full consulting-grade feasibility. |
| 4 | Product / supplier opportunity research | Repeats often and fits AI workflow, but scattered across Amazon, sourcing, arbitrage, AI tools, food-tech. Needs a niche to avoid being generic. |
| 5 | Primary interviews / survey recruitment | Real budgets exist, but geography, respondent access, and live interviewing reduce leverage. Better later, not first SKU. |

## First SKU Signal

The strongest first SKU from this sample is:

**Competitor + Market Opportunity Brief**

Suggested shape:

| Component | Scope |
|---|---|
| Buyer input | Product/service, target market, 3-10 known competitors or search terms |
| Research actions | Competitor map, pricing/offer comparison, positioning, funnel/content scan, customer pain points, gaps/opportunities |
| Deliverable | 8-12 page PDF or Google Doc plus comparison spreadsheet |
| Turnaround | 3-5 business days |
| Target budget | $200-$500 fixed price |
| Why this first | Repeated demand, report deliverable, high AI leverage, lower barrier than formal feasibility/financial modeling, less commodity than raw lead generation |
