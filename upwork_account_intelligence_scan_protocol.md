Status: NEXT SCAN — ACCOUNT RESEARCH / SALES INTELLIGENCE / PROSPECT INTELLIGENCE

Objective:

Test whether there is a standalone Upwork market for account-level sales intelligence.

This is not generic lead generation.

## Target Deliverable

For each target account:

- Company
- Decision maker
- ICP fit
- Recent trigger or buying signal
- Pain/opportunity hypothesis
- Priority score
- Reason to contact now
- Suggested outreach angle

## Include Only If

- The buyer wants intelligence, context, prioritization, or research-backed reasons.
- Outreach/calling/appointment setting is not the core deliverable.
- The output can be delivered as a spreadsheet, account brief, prospect profile, or target-account report.
- The task is closer to sales research than sales execution.

## Exclude If

- The job is only name/email/LinkedIn collection.
- The job is primarily SDR, cold calling, appointment setting, campaign management, or closing.
- The main value is Clay/Apollo/Instantly/n8n setup or automation.
- The work depends on owning a specialized paid database as the central input.

## Required Fields

- Job Title
- Description
- Deliverable
- Fixed or Hourly
- Budget
- Buyer Spend
- Buyer Hires
- Proposals
- Geography
- Expertise
- Posted Time
- Job URL
- Account Quantity
- ICP Criteria
- Decision Maker Depth
- Trigger / Buying Signal Required
- Prioritization Required
- Reason-to-Contact Required
- Outreach Execution Required
- Tool Requirement
- Price per Account / Effective Rate
- Recurring or One-off
- AI Completion Notes
- Entry Barrier Notes
- Include / Exclude Decision
- Exclusion Reason

## Decision Target

Look for evidence of a stable middle layer:

- more valuable than lead list building
- more available than broad market research
- still research/intelligence, not sales execution
- high AI leverage
- human value remains in judgment, scoring, and recommended angle
