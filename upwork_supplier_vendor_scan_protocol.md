Status: NEXT SCAN — SUPPLIER / VENDOR RESEARCH + SHORTLIST

Objective:

Test whether buyers purchase supplier/vendor sourcing research as a standalone Upwork task with enough density, acceptable pricing, high AI leverage, and manageable entry barriers.

## Target Deliverable

Buyer provides:

- product/category
- geography or sourcing region
- required specifications
- minimum order quantity or scale
- quality/certification requirements
- budget/price range, if available

Freelancer delivers:

- verified supplier/vendor shortlist
- contact details
- product/spec fit
- pricing or quote evidence, when available
- MOQ / lead time / shipping notes, when available
- comparison table
- recommendation or ranked shortlist

## Include Only If

- The job is primarily research/sourcing/shortlist creation.
- The output is an artifact: spreadsheet, supplier list, comparison table, sourcing report, or ranked shortlist.
- The buyer is not primarily asking for procurement execution, negotiation, logistics management, import/export compliance, or in-person factory inspection.
- The task can plausibly be done remotely using public sources, supplier platforms, email/contact verification, and structured comparison.

## Exclude If

- The job requires local physical inspection or in-person visits.
- The core work is purchasing/procurement operations.
- The core work is negotiation or vendor management after shortlist creation.
- The job requires specialized regulated compliance expertise.
- The task is only raw contact scraping with no qualification/comparison.

- Job Title

- Recommendation / Ranking Required
- Contact Depth
- Tool / Platform Requirement
- Recurring or One-off
- AI Completion Notes
- Entry Barrier Notes
- Include / Exclude Decision
- Exclusion Reason

## Decision Target

Look for:

- repeated standalone sourcing/shortlist jobs
- fixed-price or hourly economics above raw list building
- buyer spend/hiring history
- global/remote eligibility
- public/source-platform inputs
- meaningful comparison and recommendation value
- limited need for local identity, factory visits, regulated compliance, or logistics execution
