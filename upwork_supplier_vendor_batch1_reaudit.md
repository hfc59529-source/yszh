## Why Re-Audit Was Needed

Batch 1 included jobs that prove there is money in sourcing, but some of those jobs were not a clean fit for a low-asset, AI-leveraged research SKU.

Several included jobs were actually sourcing execution:

- Pet Wellness: negotiation, dropship terms, sample orders, QC, compliance guardrails, SLA pack.
- Turkey/Pakistan/India T-Shirt: local sourcing agent requirement.
- Golfwear: factory relationships, introductions, sampling coordination, Asia production ties.
- Mexico Agent: local supplier communication and local manufacturing network.

These prove sourcing demand, but not necessarily remote supplier research as an independent SKU.

## Revised Strict-Fit Definition

Include only when:

- Work can be completed remotely.
- Research and screening are the primary value.
- Outreach is at most light inquiry/RFQ.
- The buyer does not require local identity, factory visits, audits, QC, long-term supplier management, or negotiation as the core outcome.
- The final output is structured: supplier shortlist, quote matrix, MOQ, lead time, certifications, comparison, recommendation.
