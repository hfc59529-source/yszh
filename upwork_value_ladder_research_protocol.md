Status: NEXT RESEARCH QUESTION — VALUE LADDER

Objective:

Determine which small movement beyond pure information raises willingness to pay while preserving AI leverage.

## Value Ladder

1. Information

- Find facts.
- Build list.
- Summarize market.

2. Verification

- Confirm facts.
- Check supplier/contact/product fit.
- Cross-check claims.
- Validate with sources, screenshots, certifications, directory records, public proof.

3. Light Execution

- Send RFQs.
- Collect supplier replies.
- Request MOQ, price, lead time, certification documents, terms.
- Create comparison table.
- Summarize response quality.

4. Transaction Evidence

- Provide proof that a real party can supply, buy, distribute, or respond.
- Example: actual quote, email response, catalog proof, sample availability, buyer/supplier confirmation.

## Research Question

Which step causes the biggest price jump without turning the task into heavy execution?

## Include Task Examples If They Ask For

## Decision Target

Find whether there is a repeatable paid step between research and heavy execution.

Strong candidate pattern:

- $300-$1,000 or $20+/hr
- standalone deliverable
- light outreach or verification only
- no local identity or heavy operations
- output includes real-world proof, not just desk research

Weak pattern:

- price remains near $100
- price jumps only when execution burden becomes heavy
- proof requires local presence, negotiation, QC, sales calls, or project ownership
