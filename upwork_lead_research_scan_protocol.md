Status: SCAN IN PROGRESS — LEAD RESEARCH / QUALIFIED LEAD LIST

Objective:

Validate which layer of Lead Research is economically attractive, not whether the broad lead generation market exists.

## Layers

L1 — List Building

- Find companies
- Find names/emails
- Fill Google Sheet/CSV
- Basic LinkedIn verification

Risk:

- High density but likely low price and high competition.

L2 — Qualified Lead Research

- Filter by ICP
- Verify fit
- Confirm contact/company fields
- Remove bad or irrelevant leads

Potential:

- More valuable than raw list building if criteria are clear and buyer has repeat demand.

L3 — Account Intelligence

- Identify decision makers
- Evaluate account fit
- Find buying signals
- Prioritize accounts
- Explain why each account is worth contacting
- Support personalization

Potential:

- Strongest fit for AI leverage plus human judgment.
- Buyer is not just buying contacts; buyer is buying sales prioritization.

## Extra Fields For Clean Scan

- Lead Quantity
- Qualification Criteria
- Contact Depth
- Verification Required
- Prioritization Required
- Buying Signal Required
- Personalization Required
- Price per Lead / Effective Rate
- Recurring or One-off
- Tool Requirement
- Layer: L1 / L2 / L3

## Evaluation Target

Look for a stable pattern with:

- qualified or prioritized leads
- clear ICP
- verifiable information
- buyer repeat purchasing or hiring history
- pricing above pure list building
- AI can do most search and enrichment
- human value remains in qualification, judgment, prioritization, and explanation

Do not mark this PASS just because job volume is high.
